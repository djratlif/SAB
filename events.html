How Staff Can Update This Calendar in 15 Seconds:

No need for complicated web dashboards or fragile plugins. Here are the most relaxed, low-stress solutions available today:

📱
1. Google Calendar / iCal Sync (Top Pick)

Staff creates an event right on their iPhone/Android Google Calendar app. The website reads the feed and automatically displays it in this design. Zero passwords, zero CMS hassle.

📝
2. Notion or Airtable App

Open the free Notion/Airtable app on mobile, type the band name, upload a photo of the band or food menu, and it live-syncs directly to the website cards.

🌿
3. Simple Headless CMS

A lightweight custom editor (like Sanity.io or Netlify Decap) gives the brewery custom fields for beers and music with one-click publishing.
