The Patio & Tasting Room

Your neighborhood front porch in Downtown Hendersonville. Unpretentious, family-friendly, dog-friendly, and full of mountain laughter.

mountain sunshine

An Outdoor Mountain Beer Garden

Step through our historic warehouse doors onto our sunny outdoor patio. Framed by mountain breezes and string lights, it's the perfect spot to unwind after a hike in DuPont Forest or a stroll down Main Street.

🐾
Dog-Friendly Always

Well-behaved, leashed dogs are always welcome outside. We keep clean water bowls and plenty of shade ready for your pups.

🥨
Underground Baking Co. Pretzels

Delivered fresh locally every single day! Giant, warm, salted Bavarian pretzels served with artisan stone-ground mustard.

🎯
Lawn & Board Games

Grab a cold pint and challenge friends to cornhole, ladder golf, giant Jenga, or choose from our extensive taproom board game shelf.

Food Trucks & Refreshments

While we focus on brewing great beer, we partner with Western North Carolina's best mobile chefs. Visiting food trucks pull up Wednesday through Sunday serving tallow smashburgers, gourmet tacos, wood-fired pizzas, and smoked wings.

You are also always welcome to bring your own takeout from any of Hendersonville's great downtown eateries!
